Open Directional Sensitivity Calibration

Measures primary-submovement overshoot/undershoot across 8 counter-clockwise directions (odd-sensor-ccw) using raw Pointer Lock input.

Turn the directional curve off first. The browser sees motion after the mouse or host filter has applied it, so a calibration through an active curve is relative to it and recalibrating undoes the last one. OpenMouse: calibration bypass (bypassForCalibration()) or disable the curve; Linux: stop odd_evdev_filter; QMK and devicetree curves: flash a flat curve.
